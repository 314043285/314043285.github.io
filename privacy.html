Privacy Policy for BMS Config Tool

Last updated: 2026-10-23

BMS Config Tool is a local Bluetooth configuration tool for battery management systems.

This App does NOT collect, store, transmit or share any personal data of users.

All battery measurement data (voltage, current, temperature, status, settings) is stored locally on your iPhone only. No data will be uploaded to any remote server.

The App uses Bluetooth only to communicate with your local BMS hardware device.

We do not request, access or collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, device identifiers or any other personal information.

Your rights under GDPR: Since we collect zero personal data, there is no personal data to access, correct or delete.
